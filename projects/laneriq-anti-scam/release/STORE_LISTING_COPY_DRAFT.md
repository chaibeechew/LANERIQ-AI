# LANERIQ Anti Scam — Store Listing Copy Draft

Status: draft for review against the exact shipping feature set.

## Short description

Privacy-first anti-scam protection with Guardian integrity, suspicious-link checks, app/file risk inspection, and clear protection-state truth.

## Long description

LANERIQ Anti Scam is designed to help users recognize and interrupt scam, phishing, suspicious-app, and device-protection risks without turning security into private-content surveillance.

### Protection Guardian

A user-enabled Guardian keeps a fresh local protection heartbeat, detects when protection becomes stale or unavailable, and surfaces degraded protection instead of continuing to show a false green state.

### Suspicious-link checks

LANERIQ can inspect a destination using local phishing heuristics and privacy-minimized threat reputation. High-risk destinations can be interrupted with a warning based on available evidence. A low-risk result is not a guarantee that a site is safe.

System-wide web blocking must only be described as active in store copy after the production build contains a verified, user-consented network-protection implementation and the corresponding release evidence has passed.

### App, APK and file risk inspection

User-selected files and APKs can be fingerprinted and inspected for technical risk evidence such as signing identity, permissions, Accessibility/Device Admin/overlay capability signals, and trusted threat evidence when available. Suspicious metadata alone is not relabeled as a virus.

### Anti-remote-control risk protection

LANERIQ can combine permitted technical risk signals such as suspicious Accessibility/ADB/developer-state conditions with Guardian integrity and emergency protection logic. It can freeze LANERIQ-controlled sensitive flows when strong correlated risk or verified protection loss is present. It cannot guarantee that every form of remote compromise is impossible.

### Privacy First

The default security path is local-first. LANERIQ is designed not to upload private messages, passwords, authentication tokens, private keys, full browsing history, contacts, photos, microphone audio, clipboard contents, or screen contents as general security telemetry.

### Truth-first security

LANERIQ does not use unsupported `CLEAN`, `virus-free`, `BANKING_SAFE`, `hacker-proof`, `100% protected`, or impossible-to-stop claims. Protection states are based on current evidence and may show degraded, paused, unavailable, or review-required states when evidence is incomplete.
