# LANERIQ Cognitive OS Round 7 — 2526 Civilization Horizon

This round translates a 500-year AI horizon into present-day engineering proxies. It is not a claim that LANERIQ has 2526-era AI.

## Core idea
A 500-year system cannot assume today's model vendors, cryptography, protocols, hardware, institutions, jurisdictions, language conventions, network latency, or credentials will still exist. The durable asset must therefore be the LANERIQ-owned cognitive/authority/evidence contract rather than any current substrate.

## 24 civilization-scale capability targets
Multi-century identity continuity; constitutional policy continuity; revocable temporal goals; civilization causal world models; branchable future histories; civilization memory archives; epistemic time capsules; century-scale provenance; crypto agility; self-describing protocol evolution; substrate-independent compute; delay-tolerant cognition; distributed agent institutions; resource/energy governance; multi-jurisdiction sovereignty; embodied fleet governance; autonomous science governance; infrastructure stewardship; governed recursive improvement; dissent preservation; cultural/language continuity; catastrophe recovery bootstrap; successor-authority continuity; human sovereignty and veto.

## Temporal authority
No authorization becomes permanent merely because it is old. Grants expire, remain revocable, and must be re-issued by a verified authority. The current proxy caps one grant at 25 years and century-scale goals require review at least every 10 years. Missing authority causes dormancy/default deny rather than autonomous expansion.

## Protocol + crypto evolution
Canonical intent is kept separate from transport/protocol details. Adapters may not increase authority or raise evidence class. Cryptographic algorithms are registry-driven and replaceable, with dual-validation migration, independent validation, rollback, and no automatic downgrade. This aligns with the present-day need for crypto agility, including PQC migrations, without assuming today's algorithms remain secure for centuries.

## Civilization memory
Epistemic time capsules store digests/evidence references and migration lineage rather than raw private content. Format migrations retain the original until digest, lineage, semantic-equivalence and multi-reader checks pass. The design is tamper-evident, not claimed tamper-proof.

## Delay-tolerant cognition
The system does not assume real-time global consensus. Long-delay/disconnected nodes operate under local authority and local safety policy. High-risk remote actuation remains disabled; conflicting decisions are preserved and reconciled later, with freeze-and-escalate for high-risk conflicts.

## Catastrophe recovery
Recovery starts from default deny: verify surviving authority, restore minimal safety policy and provenance, validate time capsules, rebuild provider/compute registries, reconstruct in sandbox, require independent Judge + human successor approval, then progressively restore service. Old credentials are not trusted merely because they survived.

## Truth boundary
Round 7 is CODE + CI future-proxy architecture only. It does not claim interstellar deployment, 2526 models, future cryptographic security, civilization-scale autonomous governance, or Production self-evolution. Future capabilities remain subject to LANERIQ evidence boundaries, least privilege, independent Judge, human veto and Production Release Control.
