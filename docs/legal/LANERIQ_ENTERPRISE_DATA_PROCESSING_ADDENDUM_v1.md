# LANERIQ AI Enterprise Data Processing Addendum

**Version:** LANERIQ-DPA-v1.1-DRAFT  
**Corpus:** LANERIQ-LEGAL-CORPUS-2026.09.05-r1  
**Status:** DRAFT — QUALIFIED MALAYSIAN LEGAL/PRIVACY REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE CONTRACTUAL ACTIVATION  
**Last Harmonized:** 2026-09-05

This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is intended for Business/Enterprise customers where LANERIQ AI processes personal data on behalf of the customer in connection with the services. It does not override the parties' actual legal roles.

## 1. Roles and scope

The final order/execution record must identify for each relevant processing activity:

- the customer/controller or other applicable role;
- LANERIQ's processor/controller or other applicable role;
- data subjects and data categories;
- documented processing purposes/instructions;
- duration/retention; and
- any approved special/sensitive-data handling.

No contractual label changes a role that applicable law assigns differently based on the facts.

## 2. Documented instructions and purpose limitation

Where LANERIQ acts as processor, it will process customer personal data only on documented customer instructions and as necessary to provide, secure and support the contracted service, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

LANERIQ may refuse or pause an instruction that appears unlawful, technically unsafe, outside contracted scope or likely to expose another customer's data, platform secrets or security controls.

## 3. Confidentiality and access

Personnel and authorized service providers with access to customer personal data must be subject to appropriate confidentiality duties and least-privilege controls.

Customer data must not be used for unrelated advertising profiling or unrelated model training merely because it is processed through the service.

## 4. Security

LANERIQ should maintain risk-appropriate technical and organizational measures including, where applicable, authentication, least privilege, encryption in transit, secret isolation, environment/tenant separation, logging, vulnerability management, malware controls, backups/recovery and incident response.

No DPA statement may claim a certification, residency or security control not actually supported by Production evidence.

## 5. Subprocessors

LANERIQ may use subprocessors for hosting, AI/model processing, databases/storage, communications, analytics/monitoring, payments, security or support where appropriate.

The current Production subprocessor/provider inventory must be disclosed accurately. The final DPA must define notice/objection mechanics that LANERIQ can operationally honour and impose appropriate data-protection obligations where required by law.

## 6. International transfers

Cross-border processing/access must follow then-current Malaysian data-protection requirements and any additional mechanism contractually agreed with the customer.

LANERIQ must not promise a data-residency location unless actual routing/storage architecture supports it.

## 7. Data-subject requests

Taking into account the nature of processing, LANERIQ will provide commercially reasonable assistance for access, correction, withdrawal/deletion or other legally applicable requests where the customer cannot reasonably fulfil the request without LANERIQ assistance.

Assistance remains subject to lawful retention, security evidence, legal holds and the parties' actual statutory roles.

## 8. Personal-data breaches

LANERIQ will maintain a documented incident process and inform the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting customer data where notification is required by the final DPA or applicable law.

A security alert still under investigation must not automatically be represented as a confirmed personal-data breach.

The parties must distinguish:

- LANERIQ-to-customer contractual notification timing; and
- any statutory notification by the legally responsible controller/processor to the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Commissioner or affected data subjects.

As of the 2026-09-05 review, current Malaysian DBN guidance requires qualifying notification to the Commissioner as soon as practicable and no later than 72 hours from occurrence. The actual incident record must preserve occurrence/detection/confirmation and the legally assessed clock basis; the DPA must not hard-code `confirmation + 72h` as a universal statutory rule.

## 9. DPO and regulatory cooperation

Each party remains responsible for assessing its own statutory DPO/registration duties based on its actual processing role and facts.

Current Malaysian DPO guidance reviewed on 2026-09-05 identifies any of these as DPO triggers: personal data exceeding 20,000 data subjects; sensitive personal data including financial information exceeding 10,000 data subjects; or regular and systematic monitoring of personal data.

The customer and LANERIQ must not assume that being below a numeric threshold automatically removes DPO obligations where regular/systematic monitoring exists.

## 10. Deletion and return

At the end of the relevant service, LANERIQ will return or delete customer personal data according to the contracted retention/deletion process, subject to backups, legal holds, fraud/security evidence, tax/accounting records and other lawful retention requirements.

Deletion obligations must be technically realistic and must not require destruction of records that lawfully must be retained.

## 11. Audit and evidence

Enterprise customers may receive reasonable compliance evidence appropriate to the service. Audit rights must be proportionate, protect other customers and LANERIQ confidential/security information, and may use independent reports or structured questionnaires where appropriate.

No audit right authorizes access to another customer's data, LANERIQ private keys, exploit details, private system prompts or unnecessary source code.

## 12. Customer responsibilities

The customer remains responsible for lawful collection, notices/consents where required, documented instructions, user authorization, data accuracy, permissions and legality of the customer's application/use case.

The customer must not instruct LANERIQ to process personal data it has no lawful authority to provide.

## 13. Order of precedence

Once legally approved and executed, this DPA prevails over conflicting general privacy language only for the specific processing obligations within its scope. The main/enterprise agreement governs commercial matters not specifically modified here. Mandatory law controls where it cannot be varied by contract.

## 14. Legal review gate

Before activation, qualified Malaysian counsel/privacy professionals must confirm the final controller/processor terminology and obligations under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 as amended, current DBN/DPO/registration/cross-border guidance, subprocessor terms, retention, audit rights and any sector-specific requirements, reconciled against the actual Production data map.
