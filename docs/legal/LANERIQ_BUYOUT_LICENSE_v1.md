> This document records the intended product terms for the LANERIQ AI project-specific Buyout License. It is the current harmonized draft, but it is not an ACTIVE binding Production instrument until the Production Legal Approval Gate is satisfied for the exact version/hash.

## 1. Project-specific licence

A Buyout License applies to one identified eligible LANERIQ AI project and is recorded using a unique electronic License ID. It does not automatically apply to any other project owned by the same customer.

The Buyout is a project-specific commercial licence/path. It does not by itself sell or assign LANERIQ AI platform ownership, internal technology or unrelated intellectual property.

## 2. Prices

The current Buyout License prices are:

- Personal: **US$49**
- Business: **US$199**
- Enterprise: **US$499**

The applicable tier, Project ID, amount, currency and payment/receipt reference must be recorded before the License is issued.

## 3. Eligibility

A Buyout License must be selected before the project is published unless a later legally approved product rule expressly permits another timing.

The following are not eligible under the current product policy:

- Game projects; and
- the specific project that actually redeemed LANERIQ AI Encourage Creator support.

Other eligible non-Game projects owned by the same Creator are not disqualified merely because another project used Encourage Creator support.

## 4. Customer project rights and LANERIQ reserved technology

The customer retains ownership of the customer's project and original project materials, subject to third-party rights, open-source licences and other applicable rights.

The Buyout License does not transfer ownership of:

- the LANERIQ AI platform, brand, shared runtime or orchestration;
- LANERIQ internal services, models, system prompts, safety systems or proprietary libraries;
- provider accounts, secrets, private keys or credentials;
- another customer's project, content or data; or
- unrelated LANERIQ proprietary technology.

## 5. Source-code access

An active Buyout License unlocks the project source-code access defined by the LANERIQ AI Buyout policy for the identified eligible project. Source access remains subject to security controls and does not include another customer's data or LANERIQ AI private platform credentials.

Third-party and open-source components remain subject to their own licences and transfer restrictions.

## 6. Future LANERIQ AI revenue share after Buyout

For the project covered by an active eligible Buyout License, the intended LANERIQ AI future revenue-share rate after the effective Buyout is **0%**, subject to the final legally approved terms.

Revenue, fees or obligations validly accrued before the effective Buyout are not automatically cancelled unless the final approved terms expressly provide otherwise.

An active eligible Buyout must not be silently combined with the Project Portability / Revenue Share Agreement's 10% post-migration share for the same post-Buyout Project Software Revenue.

## 7. Stamp duty, filing and government charges

### 7.1 Commercial allocation

As between LANERIQ AI and the Buyout Customer, the intended commercial allocation is that the **Buyout Customer bears and is responsible for any stamp duty, adjudication, filing, payment and related governmental charges attributable to the Buyout instrument**, except to the extent applicable mandatory law allocates liability to another person.

This clause allocates contractual cost/responsibility only. It does not override section 33, the Third Schedule or any other mandatory provision of the Stamp Act 1949.

### 7.2 Manual-review-only rule

LANERIQ AI does **not** automatically:

- classify the Buyout instrument for stamp-duty purposes;
- calculate a stamp-duty rate;
- adjudicate or submit the instrument to HASiL;
- pay stamp duty for the Customer;
- deduct a guessed duty amount from the Buyout fee; or
- debit any Customer, LANERIQ or third-party payment method for stamp duty.

The Production stamp-duty mode for Buyout is **MANUAL REVIEW ONLY**.

LANERIQ may preserve the Buyout License, Project ID, License ID, exact document version/hash, execution date and later stamping evidence supplied by the legally responsible person.

### 7.3 No tax/stamp representation

The Buyout price does not mean that the Buyout instrument is automatically exempt, automatically chargeable, or subject to any particular stamp-duty rate. Instrument classification and statutory liability depend on the actual instrument and then-current law.

Electronic signing, payment confirmation, a LANERIQ certificate or document hash must not be represented as proof that an instrument is duly stamped.

## 8. Electronic acceptance and evidence

Buyout is a material commercial obligation and should use the strong electronic acceptance level defined by the LANERIQ Electronic Signature & Acceptance Evidence Standard and Legal Acceptance Matrix.

The execution record should preserve at least:

- authenticated customer/account ID;
- Project ID;
- License tier and price/currency snapshot;
- exact Terms Version and SHA-256 document hash;
- affirmative acceptance action;
- recent re-authentication/MFA evidence appropriate to risk;
- acceptance timestamp; and
- tamper-evident License/acceptance reference.

A photographed handwritten signature is not required by the standard LANERIQ workflow.

## 9. Electronic certificate

After payment/receipt confirmation, required acceptance and successful License issuance, LANERIQ AI creates an electronic certificate containing at least:

- unique License ID;
- Project ID and project name snapshot;
- license tier;
- license fee and currency;
- issue date/time;
- License status;
- certificate version; and
- exact terms version/hash reference.

The certificate evidences the LANERIQ Buyout License record. It is not a tax certificate and does not prove stamp-duty compliance.

The customer's Dashboard copy is the product source of truth. LANERIQ AI may also provide a transactional email copy where available. Failure or delay of an email does not invalidate an otherwise validly issued Dashboard License record.

## 10. External migration

An active eligible Buyout License satisfies the Buyout commercial path for that licensed project. A project without an active eligible Buyout License remains subject to the applicable Project Portability / Revenue Share Agreement requirements before full external migration is enabled.

## 11. Transfer or sale of the project after Buyout

A Buyout License does **not** automatically transfer to a later buyer, assignee or successor merely because the project or App is sold.

Any transfer, assumption or continuation of the Buyout status with a new project owner requires the transfer mechanism expressly allowed by the then-current legally approved Buyout terms and transaction record. In the absence of such a mechanism, LANERIQ must not invent an automatic transfer.

This rule is separate from the App Sale & IP Assignment Agreement, which transfers only the assets and rights expressly identified in that transaction.

## 12. Refunds and reversals

Refund, cancellation and chargeback treatment is governed by the LANERIQ Refund, Cancellation & Chargeback Policy and mandatory law.

No clause in this License removes a refund or consumer right that applicable law makes non-excludable. A chargeback or refund does not by itself rewrite historical legal evidence or automatically transfer ownership of unrelated assets.

## 13. Third-party components

Third-party services, open-source components, app-store accounts, domains, API accounts and non-transferable licences remain subject to their own terms. A LANERIQ AI Buyout License cannot grant rights LANERIQ AI does not own.

## 14. Order of precedence

For the project-specific Buyout subject matter, this Buyout License controls over conflicting general Platform Terms to the extent of the direct conflict. Mandatory law remains controlling.

The App Sale/IP Assignment Agreement, Data Transfer Addendum and other specifically executed documents govern their own transaction subjects and do not automatically transfer this Buyout License.
